Light Merge
Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Light Merge ("the Game") is developed by Blubalab ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the Game collects, why, and how it is used. Light Merge is a single-player, offline puzzle game. There is no account system, no chat, and no way for players to communicate with each other.

Information we collect directly

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information ourselves. All of your progress (coins, unlocked items, best runs, quest progress, settings) is saved in a single file on your own device and is never sent to us or to any server we control. Uninstalling the Game deletes this data.

The one exception is the optional gameplay statistics described under GameAnalytics below, which are sent only on Android and only if you agree to them.

Permissions the Game requests

On Android, the Game requests the VIBRATE permission, used only to play haptic feedback when objects merge. It is not used to collect any information.

On iPhone, the Game asks for permission to add photos to your library the first time you share a run's picture and choose "Save Image". This lets the Game save that picture; it cannot see or read the photos you already have. The picture stays on your device unless you choose to share it. Haptic feedback on iPhone needs no permission.

On iPhone, the Game does not ask for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency), so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is never made available to the Game or to Unity Ads.

Third-party services

The Game is built with Unity and may use the following third-party services. These services operate under their own privacy policies, linked below, and may independently collect data such as device identifiers or advertising identifiers as described in those policies.

Unity Ads (rewarded video and, where enabled, other ad formats): used to offer optional rewarded videos (for example, to continue a run or refill a booster) and to show occasional ads between runs. On first launch the Game asks whether ads may be personalised; you can change your answer at any time in Settings. See Unity's Privacy Policy.
Unity IAP: used to process the optional "Remove Ads" in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by the store you installed the Game from: Google Play on Android, or the Apple App Store on iPhone. We never see or store your payment details. See Google's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.
GameAnalytics (optional gameplay statistics, Android only): helps us see how the Game is played so we can improve it, for example how long sessions last, whether players come back, how runs end, and which features and ads are used. On Android the Game asks whether you agree right after the ads question; nothing is sent unless you choose SHARE, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings > Privacy. Choosing DON'T SHARE changes nothing else in the Game.

If you agree, GameAnalytics receives: a random identifier created for this install (not your name, account, or advertising identifier, which is never used), gameplay events such as runs started and finished with their score, continues, boosters, quests and ads watched, session start and length, and basic device information (device model, operating system version, app version, and language). GameAnalytics may use your IP address to estimate your country. The data is stored and processed by GameAnalytics on our behalf. See GameAnalytics' Privacy Policy.

The iPhone version does not include GameAnalytics and sends no statistics.

We do not use any advertising-attribution or crash-reporting SDK, and no analytics beyond the optional GameAnalytics statistics above. We do not sell any data.

Children's privacy

Light Merge is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The Game contains no chat, user-generated content, or social features of any kind.

Data retention and deletion

Because all progress data lives only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the Game. We hold nothing on our side to delete.

Gameplay statistics, if you agreed to them, are kept by GameAnalytics under its own data retention rules. They are tied only to the random identifier described above, which we cannot link to you. Choosing DON'T SHARE in Settings stops sending at once; clearing the app's data or uninstalling the Game discards that identifier, so anything sent afterwards cannot be joined to what was sent before. For any question or request about this data, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example if a future update adds a new service, we will update this page and revise the date above.
